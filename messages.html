Meet Our Leaders

Prof. P.V. Konnur

Director, Prajnavahini Global School

It is with great joy that I welcome you to Prajnavahini Global School — a nurturing space where young minds are guided to explore, express, and excel. With decades of experience in academia, having served in key positions at universities such as Goa University, Bangalore University, and Rani Channamma University, I am honoured to lead this institution into a future of meaningful and purposeful holistic education.

My continued work as President of LIS Academy has strengthened my belief in the transformative power of learning — a vision we bring to life at Prajnavahini. Situated in Sir M. Visvesvaraya Layout, Bangalore, our school offers a strong early education foundation through pre-primary programs and is expanding to include Grades 1 to 5 under the globally recognized IGCSE curriculum.

We are committed to creating a joyful, secure, and future-ready learning environment. I invite you to join us on this inspiring journey of nurturing tomorrow’s leaders.

Ms. Vidya S

Principal, Prajnavahini Global School

Dear Parents and Guardians,

Warm greetings from Prajnavahini Global School!

Vidya S is an experienced academic leader and educator with over 19 years of expertise in teaching, educational administration, teacher training, and curriculum development. She has worked as a Teacher, Academic Head, Regional Trainer, and Coordinator across reputed institutions, specializing in Montessori methods, Cambridge-aligned literacy, phonics, inclusive education, and professional development programs. She has been honored with the National Excellence Achievers Award for her outstanding contribution as an "Excellent Educationist."

Welcome to Prajnavahini Global School, located in the heart of Sir M. Visvesvaraya Layout, Bangalore, where we are dedicated to nurturing young minds through joyful, future-focused education. Our Pre-Primary program is thoughtfully designed to create a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, and confidence in young learners.

We believe that early childhood education lays the foundation for lifelong learning. Through Montessori-inspired practices, interactive activities, and holistic development programs, we focus on nurturing each child's social, emotional, intellectual, and physical growth.

At Prajnavahini, we value the strong partnership between parents and educators in shaping a child's future. Together, we aim to create happy learners who grow into confident, compassionate, and responsible individuals.

We warmly welcome you to be a part of our school family and look forward to a wonderful journey of learning and growth together.
