Our Philosophy

Vision & Mission

We believe that true education goes beyond academics — it shapes minds, values, and purpose.

Our Vision


We nurture happy, confident, and curious young learners to explore, grow, and shine in a caring environment.

Our Mission


Prajnavahini Global School provides a nurturing and innovative learning environment that fosters creativity, collaboration, and confidence, shaping compassionate and capable young learners.

Our Objectives

We aim to nurture holistic development through education and values.

1. Foster Holistic Development

We aim to nurture every aspect of a child’s growth — intellectual, emotional, physical, and ethical. Our programs are designed to develop well-rounded individuals who balance academic achievement with creativity, confidence, and compassion.

2. Cultivate Global Competence with Indian Roots

We prepare our students to engage confidently in a global environment while staying grounded in Indian values and culture. Through an internationally aligned curriculum blended with traditional wisdom.

3. Promote Inquiry, Innovation, and Critical Thinking

We encourage curiosity and questioning in every classroom. By promoting hands-on learning, research projects, and creative exploration, we help students develop problem-solving skills and the courage to innovate.

4.Ensure Inclusive and Student-Centered Learning

We believe every child learns differently. Our teaching approach is flexible, inclusive, and personalized — ensuring that every learner feels valued, supported, and motivated to reach their full potential.

How We Are Different

At Prajnavahini Global School, learning goes beyond textbooks and classrooms. Our distinctive approach combines academic excellence with real-life experiences to shape confident, compassionate, and capable individuals.

  • Family Values

    We uphold and instill strong Indian family values, nurturing and respect,empathy and a deep sense of belonging in every child

  • Project-Based Learning

    We bring learning to life through hands-on projects rooted in real-time scenarios—cooking, gardening, DIY activities and enriching field trips that spark curiosity and creativity

  • Balanced use of Technology

    We embrace technology thoughtfully—enhancing learning while ensuring children stay connected to the real world and each other. At Prajnavahini, we don’t just prepare students for exams—we prepare them for life

PGS believes in the Holistic Developement of the Child

We believe that learning should not be limited to books or only in classrooms. For holistic development of learners, it is important that they interact and build a rapport with peers and acquire important social skills. Participating in various games, competitions and events enhances collaborative and cognitive skills of students

Daycare Centre

Prajnavahini Global School is a one-step solution for all your child’s needs. We have safe, secure, and structured Day Care. The sole purpose of day care is to provide a comfortable environment for the children that enhances their learning capability without any hindrance.
