About Our School

A Few Words About the School

At Prajnavahini Global School, learning goes beyond textbooks to create meaningful, real-life experiences. We focus on shaping confident, compassionate, and capable individuals through a nurturing and inclusive environment.

Located at Sir M. Visvesvaraya Layout, Bengaluru, PGS offers a strong Integrated Pre-Primary program based on global methodologies. We encourage curiosity, independence, and foundational skills through experiential learning.

From the upcoming academic year, the school will expand to Grades 1 to 5 under the Cambridge IGCSE curriculum, promoting analytical thinking, digital literacy, and a global outlook through an engaging academic approach.

Infrastructure at Prajnavahini Global School

Infrastructure at Prajnavahini Global School

Our thoughtfully designed campus provides a secure, nurturing, and stimulating learning environment for young minds to thrive.

Child-Safe Environment

Furniture, flooring, and play equipment are designed with child safety as the top priority to ensure comfort and security.

Age-Appropriate Learning Zones

Separate, well-planned spaces for Toddlers, Nursery, and Kindergarten, designed for exploration and engagement.

Safety & Security

CCTV surveillance in classrooms and corridors, trained security staff, and regular safety drills for student well-being.

Hygiene & Health

A hygienic kitchen, clean restrooms with attendants, and a medical aid room with trained staff ensure care at every step.

Modern Classrooms

Bright, airy, and technologically integrated classrooms equipped with child-friendly teaching aids to enhance learning.

Outdoor & Play Facilities

A spacious playground designed for physical fitness, teamwork, and joyful learning through play.

How We Are Different

At Prajnavahini Global School, learning goes beyond textbooks and classrooms. Our distinctive approach combines academic excellence with real-life experiences to shapeconfident, compassionate, and capable individuals.

  • Family Values

    We uphold and instill strong Indian family values, nurturingrespect,empathy and a deep sense of belonging in every child

  • Project-Based Learning

    We bring learning to life through hands-on projects rooted in real-time scenarios—cooking, gardening, DIY activities and enriching field trips that spark curiosity and creativity

  • Balanced use of Technology

    We embrace technology thoughtfully—enhancing learning while ensuring children stay connected to the real world and each other. At Prajnavahini, we don’t just prepare students for exams—we prepare them for life

PGS believes in the Holistic Developement of the Child

We believe that learning should not be limited to books or only in classrooms. For holistic development of learners, it is important that they interact and build a rapport with peers and acquire important social skills. Participating in various games, competitions and events enhances collaborative and cognitive skills of students

Daycare Centre

Prajnavahini Global School is a one-step solution for all your child’s needs. We have safe, secure, and structured Day Care. The sole purpose of day care is to provide a comfortable environment for the children that enhances their learning capability without any hindrance.

Activity Room

The activity room provides a stimulating environment for children and aids in spontaneous learning. This area is designed to encourage students to become creative thinkers. The activity room gives real-time exposure to children to express their feelings and learn to listen to others. The core idea of this activity area is to engage the children by providing them with an environment that includes a Play area, a STEM lab, Dance and Music bring them joy, happiness and creativity
